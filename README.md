# PragyaNexa Technologies Website

A responsive one-page corporate website for PragyaNexa Technologies (OPC) Pvt Ltd.

## Files
- `index.html` — website structure and copy
- `styles.css` — responsive styling
- `script.js` — mobile menu and demo form behavior
- `assets/pragyanexa-header-logo.png` — compact header logo + PRAGYANEXA name
- `assets/pragyanexa-nameplate.png` — original supplied company nameplate

## Preview
Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Before launch
1. Replace or confirm phone/address/CIN details.
2. Add the official company email address.
3. Connect the enquiry form to a backend, Formspree, EmailJS, CRM, or your own API.
4. Add domain, analytics, privacy policy and terms as needed.
5. Optimize the logo to a transparent cropped version if desired.


## Contact form
The enquiry form is configured to send submissions to `info@pragyanexa.com` using FormSubmit.

Important: on the first live submission, FormSubmit may send an activation/confirmation email to `info@pragyanexa.com`. Open that email and confirm the form once. After activation, future website enquiries will be delivered to that inbox.

The form requires an internet connection and should ideally be tested after the site is hosted on its final domain.
